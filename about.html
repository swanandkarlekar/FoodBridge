Our Mission & Purpose

Transforming Surplus into Sustenance

FoodBridge was founded on a simple truth: wholesome food should nourish people, not fill landfills.

Our Mission

To eliminate avoidable food waste by providing an accessible, real-time logistics platform that connects surplus food providers directly with verified local charities, soup kitchens, and volunteer networks.

Our Vision

A sustainable, circular community where zero edible food is discarded, every neighborhood has reliable food security, and technology serves the public good with transparency and empathy.

The Food Waste Crisis in Numbers

Every year, billions of tons of perfectly edible food are discarded while millions of families face hunger. This imbalance is not a production issue — it is an information and logistics failure. FoodBridge provides the bridge.

Trust & Compliance

Food Safety & Good Samaritan Protection

Donors often ask: "Are we liable if someone gets sick from donated food?" The answer is clear: under the Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act, businesses and donors that donate wholesome food in good faith are shielded from civil and criminal liability.

Temperature Control

Hot foods kept at ≥ 60°C (140°F) and chilled items at ≤ 4°C (40°F) until collected.

Allergen Disclosure

Donors declare standard major allergens to protect vulnerable diners at community tables.

Sanitized Packaging

All items packed in clean, food-grade containers suitable for rapid distribution.
