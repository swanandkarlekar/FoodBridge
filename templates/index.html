Seamless 3-Step Process

How FoodBridge Works

Connecting surplus food with local hunger relief is simple, fast, and completely free for donors and nonprofit organizations.

01

List Surplus in 60 Seconds

Restaurants, bakeries, or event organizers publish details: quantity, category, packaging, dietary info, and convenient pickup deadline.

02

NGOs Claim Instantly

Nearby shelters, community soup kitchens, and charities review listings matching their serving capacity and submit a one-click pickup request.

03

Safe Pickup & Distribution

NGOs or volunteer couriers pick up the nutritious food before expiry and distribute it directly to community members facing food insecurity.

The Food Paradox

The Real Impact of Food Waste

Food waste is not just an economic loss — it is an environmental emergency and an ethical failure when families in our neighborhoods go hungry.

1.3B

Tons Wasted Annually

Roughly one-third of all food produced worldwide gets thrown away, while 800+ million people face chronic food insecurity.

8-10%

Global Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Decomposing food in landfills produces methane, a greenhouse gas 25 times more potent than carbon dioxide at trapping heat.

250 km³

Freshwater Lost

The water used to produce uneaten food could meet the domestic water needs of over 9 billion people every year.
